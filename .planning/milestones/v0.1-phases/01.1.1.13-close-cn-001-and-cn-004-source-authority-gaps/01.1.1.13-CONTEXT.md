Any needed source extension must identify immutable lane versions, exact source bytes, and provenance; the prior strategy approval does not imply that missing or altered source is acceptable.

- Never invoke the audited evaluator, parser, loader, or atom-creation functions. Use source and compiled-BEAM inspection plus uncalled fixtures. Any newly judged front already called from `lib/` stops the affected work and preserves the owner-only ADR/PR decision.
- Keep implementation and evidence contract-test-only: no `lib/`, `.github/`, `mix.exs`, lockfile, or `.credo.exs` changes in the scoped PR. Require exact-head core/floor checks, green required CI, merge, and a CI run link.
- Preserve the separate Macro caller-AST and final Credo wording checkpoints. This delivery does not change the explanation or decide a new Macro policy. Keep SEC-01 pending Phase 5 and keep GSD auto-advance disabled.
- The approved archive strategy from INT-20261030-01 is already resolved; do not ask for that approval again. This does not waive evidence requirements for newly needed source files.
