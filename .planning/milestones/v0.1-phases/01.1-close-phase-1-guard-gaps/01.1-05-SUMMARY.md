## Accomplishments

- Checkout model `checkout!/3`: `refs/tags/<tag>` detaches at the tag. A bare tag takes `refs/remotes/origin/<tag>` first, through `git checkout -B`, and otherwise detaches at the tag. Any other `ref:` gives `{:ancestry, :unmodeled_ref, ref}`.
- `gate_run/4` runs every build `run:` body before the first `mix test` step, in order, with a shared `GITHUB_OUTPUT`. It covers four cases, plus the property that an exit 0 means HEAD is the tag's commit and an ancestor of origin/main.
- `run/4` uses `bash --noprofile --norc -eo pipefail` and clears `GIT_DIR`, `GIT_WORK_TREE` and `GIT_INDEX_FILE` (IN-01).
- The fake `mix` writes fixed bytes for `hex.build --output`. The publish cases run in one-commit fixture repos, with `COMMIT` set to HEAD (or 40 zeros) and `SHA256` set to the bytes' sha256 (or 64 `d`).
- WR-01 `:step_if` and a fail-closed `@gate` pinned exactly; WR-02 secret scan over every non-step job line; WR-03 comment-tolerant `steps/1` plus `@step_counts` (build 10, publish 5, smoke 4, finalize 1); IN-02 `:job_graph` and `:checkout`.
