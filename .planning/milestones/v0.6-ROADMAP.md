## Phase 7: Guard Scope Reconciliation

**Goal:** The owner can review one current, lane-pinned guard-scope reconciliation with its complete family and complement, bounded evidence and gaps, refreshed sizing, and a clear recommendation. The core evidence and reviewed source schedule are bound to the security-patched lock.

**Depends on:** Phase 6 delivery and v0.5 closeout
**Requirements:** RSC-01, RSC-02, RSC-03, RSC-04
**Plans:** five plans, including the narrow security rebaseline inside 07-05; the five-plan ceiling was preserved.

### Success criteria

1. Record the relevant saved row/function membership and exact source identities independently for each pinned lane, identify available differences, and stop for rescoping on mismatches. (RSC-01)
2. Define one exact proposed family and its complete, disjoint per-lane complement with reproducible counts/digests, clearly distinguished from a selected or promoted code candidate. (RSC-02)
3. Record bounded lane-pinned source, caller, and delegate observations with provenance, explicit gaps, and limits that do not imply exhaustive or package-wide caller closure. (RSC-03)
4. Refresh source/context/effort sizing within inherited limits, preserve all safety stops, and end at a recommendation plus explicit owner decision before any further increment. (RSC-04)
