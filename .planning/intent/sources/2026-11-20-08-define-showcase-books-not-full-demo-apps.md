# Owner direction: showcase authored Books, not complete demo applications

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Provenance:** Direct owner message in the Fablebook planning conversation.

> “we don't have to actually build out the real full demo apps. We just need to capture all the screens and workflows and stuff in the fablebook books.”

Treat the Logistify showcase as authored Fablebook Books that capture representative screens, workflows/Journeys, state variants, and key interactions. Do not require complete logistics business applications, production backends, or fully functional demo systems unless the owner selects that scope separately. Build only the Fablebook host, authoring, and rendering support needed to inspect and explain the Books.

Keep authored source brand books distinct from applied design canon and from Fablebook-rendered Books. This direction narrows the future showcase outcome; it does not waive the open guard-aggregate prerequisite, authorize a milestone, or create executable requirements or phase plans.
