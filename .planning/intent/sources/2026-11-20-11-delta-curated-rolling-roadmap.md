# Owner direction: curate the rolling roadmap by delta

**Date:** 2026-11-20  
**Provenance:** Direct owner message in the Fablebook planning conversation.

> “i hope u remember it i dont want to re-litigate this every new milestone of course we will update the roadmap and scope out the immediate next milestone each time so we are constantly curating-updating but not totally from scratchy more like a delta”

At each milestone start, treat the last owner-reviewed rolling roadmap and preserved high-signal research as the baseline. Reconcile the current checkout, delivery receipts, new owner direction, changed evidence, typed gates, and relevant source freshness; update only the parts these deltas affect. Then recommend the immediate bounded milestone and refresh the directional future order. Do not repeat the entire product-purpose debate or re-litigate settled priorities without new evidence that materially changes them.

Keep original research and intent files immutable and linked for provenance. Mark findings as still valid, changed, superseded, or newly learned; do not overwrite the prior evidence. Reopen the whole roadmap only when changed evidence undermines core assumptions or the owner requests a ground-up review. Future horizons remain directional until selected, and this practice does not bypass typed prerequisites or milestone approval boundaries.
