## Later product direction

- Keep the Fablebook workbench chrome neutral while dogfooding Fablebook's own identity on its appropriate surfaces.
- Use Logistify as the first adopter example after catalog and canon foundations. It is one brand family with three separate Books: operator console, shipper portal, and driver app. Start with the operator console, then carry shared brand rules into the portal and driver app without collapsing their different jobs and constraints.
- Make the catalog model concrete across jobs/personas, product language, cases and state coverage, ordered UI Journeys, Screens, and components. Source `flows` and `cases` are mapping inputs, not an approved Fablebook schema. In Fablebook's vocabulary, a Journey is an ordered sequence of UI steps; Logistify's Route is a shipment-domain object and must not be mapped to a Journey by name.
- Place Chatly and Clinicly after the Logistify family and canon foundations, then continue the existing dependency order for bundles, checks, motion, installer, hardening, remaining twins, dogfooding, and clearance.
- Before brand integration, make exact source versions reproducible from pinned, hash-checked JSON, tokens, HTML, and records where present. Do not rely on a mutable “latest” fetch or an ignored local checkout as the only source. Keep artwork, fonts, reuse rights, and name clearance open until destination and terms are reviewed.
