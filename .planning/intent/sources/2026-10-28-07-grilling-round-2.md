**Resolutions:**
- **Tiers (Q1):** four tiers: Primitive · Pattern · Area · Screen. The **Shell** is an Area. **Sections** are the sidebar's product folders, not a tier. This supersedes the six-tier choice in INT-20261028-05.
- **Sidebar (Q2):** tier first, then product nouns.
- **Persona/job requirement (Q3):** required on Areas and Screens. A scenario may cite **one or more jobs**, so a screen can serve several jobs.
- **Languages (Q4):** no pseudo "ghost speak" in the default UI. Real languages come from the host's Gettext locales and from twin data (e.g. de, fr, es, ja, ar). RTL is built in. A readable "Text expansion" simulation (+35%, made by repeating the string's own words) is available for stress tests.
- **Twins (Q5):** each scenario cites a chosen twin; twins are never multiplied across all scenarios. Twin spread per Screen is reported for information only. Fablebook's own repo gets a set of fictional, realistic products from different industries (see round 3).
- **Enforcement (Q6):** best practices are enforced by default (shift left), with a named, reasoned escape hatch wherever an honest need exists. A Screen's or Area's subject is a presentational function component.
- **Internal names (Q7):** term internal names get a leak check only.
- **Canon vs Book (Q8):** canon is the app's design knowledge (`design/`), and a Book is one workbench. An app can have several Books; they share the app's canon by default, or a Book can point at its own.
