# v0.6 Phase 7: guard scope reconciliation — research synthesis

**Status:** Completed before milestone activation, 2026-11-19. This is upstream recommendation research, not a plan or implementation authorization.

## Recommendation

Run one bounded, read-only Phase 7 to rejoin the current complete relevant row/function membership and source identities across both pinned lanes; propose one exact candidate family and enumerate its full complement; record bounded caller/delegate evidence and explicit unknowns; and refresh source, context, and effort sizing. End with the evidence and one owner decision about whether to authorize any next guard increment. Do not automatically chain increments.

This is the most efficient next step because v0.5 preserved an exact prior complement but did not make it current evidence or select a candidate. A fresh reconciliation prevents planning against stale membership or identities. It is a gate-enabling evidence milestone, not visible Fablebook product progress. The owner has already selected v0.6 and global Phase 7 and requested GSD-autonomous execution within the project gates.

## Project evidence and current product reality

- v0.5 delivered four reviewed EX-177 provenance observations and a checker for their exact 9,712-row complement, plus archive/test/closeout work. It did not close the guard aggregate, select a candidate, authorize implementation, or unlock the Walking Skeleton.
- The guard-aggregate gate remains open; it blocks the Walking Skeleton. An increment can close only its selected slice. The full original aggregate plus installed-wide acceptance is needed before the Walking Skeleton.
- Fablebook's sourcebook v2.1 is pinned and read in `.planning/references/fablebook-brand-intake.md`; the source assets remain staged and unapplied. Logistify v1.0 is captured with source hashes and recommendations in `.planning/references/logistify-brand-intake.md`, but its source files are absent from current `main` and no Logistify Fablebook Book or scenarios are implemented.
- The preserved snapshot contains a static brand preview, not an importer contract or runnable package UI. The current package router still serves a placeholder, so there is no Fablebook application to open as a live demo. Do not confuse brandbooks/intakes with applied canon, Fablebook Books, or runnable demos.
- No matching milestone seed adds scope. The existing roadmap already places Walking Skeleton before canon/UI content; the v0.6 guard milestone does not reorder that typed prerequisite.

## Scope contract for v0.6 / Phase 7

**Deliver:** current full membership and pinned source identities for the relevant rows/functions on both lanes; one exact proposed candidate family and full complement; bounded source/caller/delegate evidence with gaps; fresh sizing under inherited limits; clear stop/owner decision record.

**Do not:** run audited APIs or other runtime probes; consume R9; do package-wide caller searches or claim exhaustive closure; resolve actual-library-collision risk; decide candidate fit or promote a candidate; implement guards or product UI; close the aggregate; start the Walking Skeleton; move/copy brand assets or produce Books/scenarios.

**Inherited bounds:** up to 100,000 planning tokens; at most five exact source files per task; 2–3 tasks per plan; at most five plans per phase; roughly three working days per plan. Preserve the actual collision owner stop, candidate null, implementation unauthorized, aggregate open, R9 denied/unconsumed, Macro caller-AST and explanation gaps, SEC-01 Pending, legacy digest unresolved, and automatic follow-up disabled. The v0.5 no-source-reread limit is relaxed only for the narrow fresh, read-only reconciliation named above.

## Rolling product outlook

1. **Immediate — v0.6 Phase 7:** read-only reconciliation; stop at a clear owner decision. It does not create a demo UI.
2. **Near — separate owner decision:** if the evidence justifies it, authorize one guard increment at a time until the entire guard aggregate and installed-wide acceptance pass. Then build the Walking Skeleton and answer the browser-runner question.
3. **Next visible product path:** Story model/catalog, deterministic render seam and rules, typed controls/URL state and the first useful bundle, canvas/host fidelity and installer, then neutral Workbench foundations. Keep each phase small and demonstrable.
4. **Brand content:** canonicalize source custody and resolve mappings when prerequisites permit; build the Fablebook brand canon and three distinct Logistify Books (operator console, shipper portal, driver app) in the existing dependency order. Treat Chatly and Clinicly as later content. Brand sourcebooks are already valuable inputs but do not imply these Books exist.
5. **Later:** checks and visual baselines, motion, context bundles, installer/DX, remaining twins, then real adopter dogfood and clearance in their existing typed order.

This rolling view is directional only; future milestones have no executable phase plans or new phase numbers until activated.

## Research method and limits

Three independent read-only work streams examined milestone assumptions, runnable demo readiness, and primary-source technical guidance. Their findings converged on the bounded reconciliation above. The official OTP/Mix sources were also checked directly. No web research was needed for UI design because Phase 7 contains no UI scope. This research does not establish runtime safety, actual collision status, user acceptance of candidate fit, or exhaustiveness of caller evidence.
