## Common Pitfalls

### The observed `list_to_atom/1` edge is mistaken for caller control

**What goes wrong:** The reviewer makes a disposition from a graph edge rather than tracing the exact input path.  
**Why it happens:** The target function has an incoming caller graph and an atom-sink edge, but this alone does not establish whether input is caller-controlled.  
**How to avoid:** Inspect complete `asn1ct_func.erl`, every selected caller span, and the `gen_server` callback path separately on both lanes.  
**Warning signs:** Disposition language based only on Xref, names, fixed values, or the existence of a sink. [VERIFIED: sizing artifact `:13-15,36-54`]

### Source batches omit part of the dispatch path

**What goes wrong:** Source reviews cover direct graph callers but miss generated or callback-mediated calls.  
**How to avoid:** Keep all 25 external callers, complete target, connected callback binding/startup/call/cast/termination functions, gen/proc_lib bridge and RTT dependency/data-shape evidence. Prior-bound cross-references are explicit reuse. [VERIFIED: corrected sizing allocation]

### Archive additions leave stale citation pins

**What goes wrong:** New archive bytes are present but digest assertions or manifest checks still refer to the old archive.  
**How to avoid:** Make archive identity, manifest, resolver checks, and citations part of integration acceptance; compare every lane against the source sizing artifact. [CITED: `.planning/phases/01.1.1.17-bound-asn-1-external-transform-exclusions/01.1.1.17-RESEARCH.md`]

### Duration is inferred from token ratio

**What goes wrong:** Source-token arithmetic becomes a supposedly measured duration.
**How to avoid:** Treat the estimate below as an estimate; record actual task start/end intervals and re-size if the remaining work approaches three working days per plan. Phase 01.1.1.16 and .17 are only two comparison observations with different review shapes. [VERIFIED: sizing artifact `:54`; `.16` summary `:65-71`; `.17` summary `:19-23,40-42`]
