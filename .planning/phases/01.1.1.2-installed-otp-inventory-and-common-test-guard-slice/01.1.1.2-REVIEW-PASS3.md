### WR-01: Lane source checks are not tied to the exclusion citation record

**File:** `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs:63-81`, `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs:2171-2201`, `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs:2802-2810`, `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs:4179-4181`

**Issue:** `@escript_debug_exclusion` stores `@escript_debug_citations`, but the new test validates a separately constructed `expected_citations` list and never compares its citation paths with the exclusion's list. The general exclusion audit only checks that citation strings match the path-and-line format; it does not resolve them against the pinned source. As a result, the record can contain stale or unrelated but syntactically valid citations while the independent source assertions continue to pass. That leaves the safety disposition's attached audit evidence unverified.

**Fix:** Compare the record's citation paths with the expected citation set for both exact lanes, then resolve the expected lane-specific entries as the test already does. Prefer defining the lane-specific citation expectations once and deriving both the exclusion list and the assertions from that shared structure, with an assertion that the combined record equals the two lane sets.
