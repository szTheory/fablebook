## Narrative Findings (AI reviewer)

### WR-01: Logger/ExUnit audit derives its completeness boundary from the records under test

**Classification:** WARNING  
**File:** `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs:1120-1131, 1320-1322, 2794-2800, 2850-2879`

**Issue:** `@logger_family_functions`, `@logger_expected_ids`, and `@logger_expected_mfas` are all derived from `@logger_family_specs`. `assert_logger_audit!/5` then restricts reached exports to that spec-derived function set before comparing them with the records. This makes the specification its own completeness baseline. Reproduction: remove a row such as `Logger.Formatter.new/1` from `@logger_family_specs` while leaving its `BannedCalls` tier in place. The generated audit and expected ID/MFA sets both lose the row, and `live_family` filters it out; the general sweep still accepts the remaining tier as a disposition. The suite can therefore pass without the required semantic record for that family MFA. The existing omitted-record controls mutate records against the current spec and do not detect this self-omission.

**Fix:** Establish the expected Logger/ExUnit export/MFA universe independently of `@logger_family_specs` for each pinned lane, then compare that universe against the specs and records. Build the live-family set from that independent boundary rather than filtering reached exports by names taken from the specs.
