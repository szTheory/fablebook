## Calibration and complete-corpus contract

The first completed named family supplies observed source/function/trace/fixture/context cost. Installed discovery records the full app/BEAM set independently of Mix pruning or app starts and records unreadable, no-debug and Xref failures. Use that data to select bounded source-family audit slices; never use row count alone. A large original matcher can span child records keyed by original identity + lane + MFA/source function. Their union must equal the original match set, including overlaps; no matched function is capped or dropped.

For inventory rollout, distinguish discovered inventory, successfully inspected inventory and active disposition coverage. Existing live sweep coverage cannot shrink. A family enters active full-sweep coverage only with all its exposed fronts disposed; pending families are recorded as pending transferred scope, never as safe exclusions or a successful full sweep. If existing graph coupling prevents a family from merging green independently, split the coupled source path together or stop that concrete slice; do not add an exemption to hide the red result. Full installed equality and zero-undisposed assertions are mandatory in the final inventory child and again at global closure.

For every audited family: source review establishes semantic truth; automation establishes strict triples, exact source identity/containment/line validity, original-to-active and per-lane matched-set equality, explicit export/inventory absence evidence and independent removal witnesses. Removal is against the complete active disposition set; remove/merge/narrow redundant matchers. Keep one graph per lane and no recurring network fetch, app starts, dangerous fixture execution, new framework or stale cross-lane cache. The final global gate has no pending/slice selector and rejects any missing original, matched MFA, source, verdict or witness.

Macro.expand/2's AST boundary remains an actual owner decision before changing its exclusion/tier. A newly banned lib caller triggers the exact MFA/callsite/input/guard report and stops its affected work; only the owner chooses an ADR-backed allowlist or a separate lib PR. Schedule this within a substantive AST-family audit child of 01.1.1.4, not a standalone decision phase. Independent families may proceed without claiming closure.
