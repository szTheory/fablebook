### Caller origin, dynamic targets, and narrow remedies

`int:eval/3 -> dbg_debugged:eval/3 -> dbg_ieval:eval/3` preserves the caller's module/function/arguments. `dbg_ieval:eval/3` either sends a re-entry message to the existing Meta or starts the fixed local Meta worker; `meta/5 -> eval_mfa/5 -> do_eval_function/6 -> get_function/4` checks interpreted clauses and export state before the explicit not-interpreted path. The top-level `dbg_apply`, nested apply command, `expr/3` remote/apply forms, and safe-BIF helper all retain unresolved dynamic targets. The narrow remedy is to keep exact interpreter-entry guards and constrain module/function/arity at each apply boundary before relaxing them. The safe-BIF and guard `apply/3` sites also need a source-derived exact allow-list; their helper names do not prove that the target is safe.
