# Debugger Source Audit

## Scope and verdict

This audit covers the assigned `dbg_idb`, `dbg_istk`, and `dbg_wx_interpret` source family on the exact core and floor installations. It does not claim debugger-wide or installed-OTP completion. The selected-file front remains `dbg_wx_interpret:start/4`; the additional exported stack decoder is independently tiered as `dbg_istk:from_external/1` and listed as an explicit source root, regardless of whether the graph reaches it.

`from_external/1` accepts `{stack, Stk}` and immediately calls `binary_to_term(Stk)` before writing the result into the process dictionary. The exported clause does not validate the caller's binary or pass a safe-decoding option. An external caller therefore is not constrained by the fact that the debugger's observed producer serializes its own stack with `term_to_binary/1`.

## Ownership and limits

This plan owns the exact `dbg_istk:from_external/1` front and the complete source dispositions for the three assigned modules. `dbg_ieval` is cited only for the consumer/producer boundary; its other routes remain with their existing source-family owners. Supporting wx module roots, including the complete `dbg_wx_mon_win:handle_event/2` callback and synthetic event/keyboard inputs, remain owned by Phase 01.1.1.3.1. Fixed GUI labels and the module list are provenance observations, not blanket callback-safety evidence.

Static tiers establish that both guards detect these source forms. They do not execute arbitrary bytes, prove runtime atom limits, or complete SEC-01. SEC-01 remains Pending for Phase 5.
