## Implementation Decisions

Inherited D-01–D-14 from 01.1.1.1, their adopted structural split and the verified inventory remain controlling. The decisions below apply the owner's request to consider all relevant alternatives and adopt coherent recommendations. They do not renew budgets or waive owner checkpoints.

### Calibrated source families and complete ownership

- **D1113-01: Partition by connected caller-input/source chains, with installation identities as the ledger.** App boundaries help route ownership but do not justify app-wide exclusions or an app-sized executable phase. Mechanism categories alone can cut wrappers away from their sinks. Each record retains lane, app, module, function/arity and caller-chain identity. Shared source/sink evidence may be referenced by multiple chains; give each canonical work item one accountable child and retain every chain/overlap relation.
- **D1113-02: Calibrate the debugger interpreter path first.** Begin source reconnaissance with the explicitly named `dbg_idb` and `dbg_istk` remainder and follow their actual connections into interpreter/load fronts. They total 25 inspected functions and 9,159 core / 9,000 floor source bytes in the final receipts, but have zero pending-MFA rows. That absence is not a safety verdict. Include a real remaining front/disposition and its recurring guard checks in the first executable family; do not create a receipt-only child. Reuse unchanged prior `i`, `int`, `dbg_iload` and `dbg_ieval` evidence where applicable, while independently resolving any unreviewed paths. All 24 debugger modules are a routing bucket, not an approved bounded phase. If the connected closure is too large, split before planning execution.
- **D1113-03: Build and verify the exact ownership manifest before plans.** Derive the expected universe independently from installed modules, full BEAM/export/reached/boundary records and source reconnaissance, not just existing tiers, reviewed specifications or a finite name filter. Assign every pending app/module/MFA/caller chain, including modules with zero graph hits, to a named bounded child or an explicit unresolved source task owned by one. Prove union equality, no dropped/duplicate canonical work items, and explicit cross-family dependencies. Changed inventory must extend this manifest; never freeze today's app count. Existing active scope cannot shrink.
- **D1113-04: Materialize a substantive bounded rollout.** Use actual source/function/chain/probe/review/context measurements and prior named-family costs. Every executable phase has at most five plans/about three working days; each plan has 2–3 tasks, at most five files per task and projected executor context at most 100,000 of 200,000 tokens. These are limits, not a promise that the whole remainder fits. Before executable PLAN files, narrow the reservation and insert adjacent children through GSD roadmap handlers, preserving dependencies into .4/.5. The final .3 child must own substantive remaining coverage plus the global installed-scope gate, not bookkeeping alone.

### Caller influence and source-backed semantic truth

- **D1113-05: Review input domains and complete routes rather than function names.** Follow caller-selected text, AST, files, filenames, settings/options, module/library names, paths and deferred messages to the sensitive behavior. Include all exported/default/option branches and relevant wrappers, process callbacks and cross-app chains. An AST evaluator is still an evaluation boundary without a string parser; a path mutator can enable later implicit loading without loading immediately. Distinguish parsing/atom creation, returning compiled output, interpretation, loading and code-path changes. Preserve existing conservative bans; no new blanket denial or safety policy is inferred merely from the words `compile`, `callback`, `internal` or `pure_data`.
- **D1113-06: Use exact-lane source evidence.** Keep per-front caller-input traces, explicit verdicts and resolvable app-relative source citations on core Elixir 1.20.4 / OTP 29.1.2 and floor Elixir 1.18.5 / OTP 27.3.4.9. Lane absence needs inventory/export evidence. Public documentation informs discovery; it does not replace exact-lane source. Reuse the approved resolver/archive and provenance/containment/digest/line checks. Missing source mappings must be resolved or kept explicitly blocked for the affected slice; no empty graph or missing citation is a benign verdict. Archive approval does not authorize unlimited new source downloads/storage.
- **D1113-07: Audit changed exclusions substantively without absorbing .4.** Every changed exclusion is a cited triple, mapped to its complete changed-family match set and preserved original identities. Each retained exclusion has an individual nonvacuous removal witness against the complete active disposition set on every applicable lane. Narrow/merge/remove overlaps; safe defaults cannot justify a blanket matcher for unsafe options. Cross-family overlap must be reported. Keep coupled paths together when needed for an independently green merge; otherwise leave the concrete slice pending, never exempt it to hide a failure.

### Static detection and truthful acceptance

- **D1113-08: Mirror unsafe fronts with uncalled probes.** Use the existing Credo and BEAM tiers, generated probes, direct calls, captures, `apply` and relevant quoted/module-body/source-only forms. Pin meaningful default/option and deferred-wrapper cases where they affect detection. Preserve benign controls, individual tier-removal witnesses and known strictness/static limitations. Fixtures contain dangerous calls in unexecuted bodies; compile/inspect them without invoking evaluators, loaders, GUI/debugger/Common Test apps or caller-selected code. Static success is not runtime atom safety or a complete computed-dispatch proof.
- **D1113-09: Separate graph membership, inspection and semantic completion.** `active_app` rows describe graph membership including Elixir apps and production dependencies; their count cannot establish completed OTP semantic review. Compare typed identity sets, not counts or a raw mixed graph-app set. A bounded child may pass its complete assigned slice while the installation remains pending. Pending is an explicit transfer with an owner, never a safe exclusion or a full-sweep pass. The prior `COVERAGE.md` is only an API-integration note and is not an ownership ledger.
- **D1113-10: Derive coverage independently and fail closed.** Reuse the full lane graph/source/export universe to challenge the reviewed manifest and family specifications. Missing/duplicate/unexpected ownership, missing fronts/traces, incorrect lane absence, omitted apps/BEAMs, failed Xref ingestion, no debug info and unresolved required source must be observable failures at the appropriate gate. Include negative controls that remove an assigned record or introduce a new export/root/caller path so a self-consistent but incomplete specification cannot pass. Newly discovered uncertainty stays owned and unresolved; do not claim static analysis enumerates every possible runtime dispatch.
- **D1113-11: Final installed-scope acceptance is unconditional.** The final .3 child requires installed OTP versus semantically swept OTP app identity equality, discovered/inspected/ingested BEAM identity equality and zero undisposed reached/boundary fronts across the full installation on both lanes. No pending/slice selector may turn that final gate green. Phase .5 independently rechecks these guarantees along with its whole-table requirements. Earlier family receipts cannot substitute for this final acceptance.

### Efficient delivery, diagnostics and existing stops

- **D1113-12: Reuse existing tools and one graph per lane.** Use ExUnit, Xref, BeamWalker, InstalledOtp, SweepEvidence and Credo, with small local helpers. Stream/index existing receipts for planning rather than dumping them into agent contexts. Reuse full/active/family projections within a lane; do not introduce a general taint framework, new dependency, recurring network fetch, application starts or stale cross-toolchain graph cache. Keep new recurring checks in existing CI lanes. Diagnostic failures should name lane, app/module/MFA, caller chain, missing or conflicting evidence, owning child and corrective action.
- **D1113-13: Preserve scoped green delivery and finite reviews.** Sequential guard PRs exclude `lib/`, `.github/`, dependencies, lockfiles and `.credo.exs` changes. Every substantive child merges only with existing required checks green and a CI link for the exact tested head. Keep local results distinct from CI and preserve exact runtime/lock/source identities. Reuse historical evidence only for unchanged behavior; refresh evidence when its guarded inputs change. The ordinary two-review-pass cap applies; earlier one-off allowances were phase-specific and are not renewed by this discussion.
- **D1113-14: Preserve owner decisions and GSD boundaries.** A newly banned `lib/` caller stops affected work with its exact MFA, callsite, caller-input trace and triggering guard; only the owner chooses the ADR-backed allowlist or separate-library-PR remedy. Macro caller-AST classification stays with the substantive .4 AST family. Changed guard explanation approval stays with .5. Continue independent authorized work without pretending a blocked slice is closed. GSD auto-advance remains disabled; this discussion ends with the research/planning command, not implementation.

### Agent's Discretion

Choose a small ledger representation, helper boundaries, probe grouping, source lookup mechanics and precise family/PR ordering after calibration. Prefer one machine-readable ownership authority with human summaries derived from it; avoid a second independently maintained list. Pure Elixir data beside the existing suite or a small structured artifact are both acceptable when independently checked. No product UI, public API or new dependency is needed. The relevant human jobs are maintaining the toolchain, reviewing precise security dispositions and getting an actionable failure when coverage changes.
