## Source and path controls

- Archive records bind each lane and source path to immutable provenance, exact byte count, and SHA-256. Archive members are checked for duplicate names, links, non-regular types, unsafe paths, inventory mismatch, and digest mismatch before use.
- Citation parsing accepts only relative app `src/` or `lib/` paths with positive line numbers. Source lookup checks lane identity, regular-file readability, resolved real-path containment, content digest, and line bounds. Equal bytes across lanes do not merge their identities.
- The TAP adapter is loaded only when the exact `Fablebook.Test.ExUnitTapFormatter` Mix formatter is selected. With the normal formatter, the helper does not load it or change ExUnit configuration. The report destination is an explicit test-runner setting and is write-only; it is not read as source or used to classify RED.
