## Review and resource bounds

The reviewer must be independent of the implementation author and receives the scope, locked decisions, exact artifact identities and required checks. Read-only review and fixes run serially to avoid shared-file races. Review is capped at two passes by ADR 0021; unresolved material findings after that limit go to the owner with the concrete consequence and remedy, not another automatic loop. Use the existing approved code/security review workflow when available; no new review framework or external service.

Each task may read the collector and tagged contract as two exact source files, leaving at most three named exact evidence sources before reaching five. Charge rereads and all required review inputs. Preserve the 31,000-token fixed reserve and 76,000 raw context projection; stop before 100,000. Review full machine records with structural queries and exact-set checks; inspect all source/caller coverage statuses and gap rules, then cite particular source evidence where needed. Querying large evidence on disk does not excuse dropping original identities or relevant sources. Effort allocation: 3–4 hours for independent review, one correction/retest pass and report publication; record actual intervals and resize before bound violations.
