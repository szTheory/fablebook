### Fast feedback boundary and tag isolation

Plan a separate `Fablebook.Test.ParserTokenizerEvidenceTest` ExUnit module inside the existing `test/contract/tier_sweep_test.exs`. Its newly added fast cases alone receive per-case `@tag :parser_tokenizer_011116`; the existing module, historical cases and comprehensive graph/guard cases remain untagged by this new tag. The new module must not depend on the original unconditional full-graph `setup_all`, build the graph, run xref/installed-wide discovery, or invoke audited ASN.1 functions/atom sinks. Both modules consume the same phase review records through a single test-only `records/0` accessor, preserving one evidence authority.

The fast commands prove exact archive/source/citation integrity and complete structured `source_only` review records, valid measured review intervals, local selected-record/disposition-schema integrity, and their local corruption/omission controls. They do **not** prove actual full-graph equality, complete live caller sets, full-active-set matcher necessity/removal, or guard detection. Those assertions remain in the unchanged comprehensive focused/full commands and are mandatory after each relevant task/final integration. The executor substantively reviews complete source and records its semantic and nonexecution evidence; tests validate the resulting records without claiming to independently prove their semantics.

Read-only planning verification found zero occurrences of `parser_tokenizer_011116` under `test/`. Existing `@tag :cn001` / `--only cn001` receipts and other tagged subset commands establish the repository convention. Pinned Elixir 1.20.4 and 1.18.5 `Mix.Tasks.Test` sources document `--only` as selecting matching tags, excluding untagged cases, and failing when no tests execute. `bin/lane` forwards the filter to Mix. During execution, verify that nonzero new tagged cases ran and no historical/full-graph cases were selected; measure latency rather than assuming the filter alone removes setup cost. No tests were run for this planning revision.
